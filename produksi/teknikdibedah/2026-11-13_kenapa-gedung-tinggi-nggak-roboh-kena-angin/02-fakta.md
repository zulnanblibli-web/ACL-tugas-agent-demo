# FACTS: Kenapa gedung tinggi nggak roboh kena angin? (@teknikdibedah #001)

Model di film adalah **gedung ilustrasi** (bukan gedung tertentu). Angka contoh diambil dari Taipei 101 dan disebut di layar sebagai contoh.

| # | Klaim di film | Sumber | Kutipan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Untuk gedung sangat tinggi, beban angin biasanya penentu utama desain; tekanan angin makin besar di ketinggian | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyscraper (diakses 2026-11-13) | "the lateral wind load imposed on supertall structures is generally the governing factor in the structural design. Wind pressure increases with height" |
| 2 | Dinding kaca (curtain wall) bukan penahan beban; ia meneruskan dorongan angin ke rangka lewat sambungan di lantai/kolom | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtain_wall_(architecture) | "They transfer lateral wind loads to the main building's structure through connections at floors or columns." |
| 3 | Rangka outrigger mengikat inti di tengah ke kolom luar | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyscraper | "the tube structure is connected to the central core wall with very stiff outriggers and belt trusses" |
| 4 | Taipei 101: rangka outrigger tiap delapan lantai menyambung kolom inti ke kolom luar | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_101 | "outrigger trusses, located at eight-floor intervals, connect the columns in the building's core to those on the exterior" |
| 5 | Taipei 101: bandul baja 660 ton sebagai peredam massa, digantung dari lantai 92 ke 88 | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_101 | "a 660-tonne steel pendulum that serves as a tuned mass damper ... Suspended from the 92nd to the 88th floor" |
| 6 | Bandul itu bisa mengurangi sampai 40% gerakan gedung | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_101 | "It can reduce up to 40% of the tower's movements." |
| 7 | Bola bandul berdiameter 5,5 m | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_101 | "welded together to form a 5.5 m diameter ball" |
| 8 | Prinsip peredam massa: massa yang frekuensi ayunnya disetel mirip frekuensi gedung, mengurangi simpangan maksimum | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuned_mass_damper | "Its oscillation frequency is tuned to be similar to the resonant frequency of the object it is mounted to, and reduces the object's maximum amplitude" |
| 9 | Taipei 101: 380 tiang fondasi ditanam 80 m ke dalam tanah | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_101 | "The foundation is reinforced by 380 piles driven 80 m into the ground" |

Tidak ada angka lain di film. Goyangan gedung di film **diperbesar** supaya terlihat, dan ditulis di layar.

| 11 | Angin di ketinggian lebih kencang (tekanan angin naik seiring ketinggian) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyscraper | "Wind pressure increases with height" |
