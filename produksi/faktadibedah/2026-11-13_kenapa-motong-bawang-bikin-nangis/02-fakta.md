# FACTS: Kenapa motong bawang bikin nangis? (@faktadibedah #001)

Semua adegan adalah **ilustrasi 3D** (bukan rekaman mikroskop). Bentuk sel, enzim, dan molekul disederhanakan.

| # | Klaim di film | Sumber | Kutipan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Memotong bawang merusak sel, dan sel yang pecah melepaskan enzim aliinase | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion (diakses 2026-11-13) | "chopping an onion causes damage to cells which releases enzymes called alliinases" |
| 2 | Aliinase mengubah sulfoksida jadi asam sulfenat | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion | "These break down amino acid sulfoxides and generate sulfenic acids." |
| 3 | Enzim kedua (LFS) mengubahnya jadi gas syn-propanethial-S-oxide | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion | "rapidly acted on by a second enzyme, the lacrimatory factor synthase (LFS), producing the syn-propanethial-S-oxide" |
| 4 | Gas itu menyebar di udara, sampai ke mata, dan mengaktifkan saraf | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion | "This gas diffuses through the air and soon reaches the eyes, where it activates sensory neurons." |
| 5 | Kelenjar air mata mengeluarkan air mata untuk mengencerkan dan membilas zat itu | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion | "Lacrimal glands produce tears to dilute and flush out the irritant." |
| 6 | Tips: potong di bawah air mengalir atau di baskom berisi air | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion | "Eye irritation can be minimised by cutting onions under running water or submerged in a basin of water." |
| 7 | Tips: sisakan pangkal akarnya, karena di situ senyawa belerangnya lebih banyak | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion | "Leaving the root end intact also reduces irritation as the onion base has a higher concentration of sulphur compounds" |
| 8 | Ini mekanisme pertahanan bawang | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion | "a chain of reactions which serve as a defence mechanism" |
| 9 | Reaksi baru mulai saat sel rusak (bawang utuh tidak melepaskan gas) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syn-Propanethial-S-oxide | "The release from onions is due to the breaking open of the onion cells, which releases enzymes called alliinases." |
