# Material sources

Downloaded with TLS verification from the official three.js repository. Files are served locally and need no CDN at runtime.

- `wood-color.jpg`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/textures/hardwood2_diffuse.jpg
- `wood-bump.jpg`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/textures/hardwood2_bump.jpg
- `grass-color.jpg`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r170/examples/textures/terrain/grasslight-big.jpg
- `daylight.hdr`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r170/examples/textures/equirectangular/royal_esplanade_1k.hdr (Royal Esplanade environment distributed with the three.js examples; original HDRI Haven / Poly Haven resource)

The three.js MIT notice is included as `LICENSE-three.txt`. Stone color/bump and the scene geometry are generated in this project. Character portraits are from the ZIP supplied by the user; characters are custom volumetric interpretations rather than mesh extractions from those PNGs.
